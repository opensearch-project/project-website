description: |
    Desktop GUI for Amazon OpenSearch Service and Amazon DynamoDB, for macOS (Apple Silicon), Windows, and Linux. Connects to OpenSearch Service domains and Serverless collections with your AWS credentials, or to a self-hosted OpenSearch endpoint such as http://localhost:9200. Search indices with Lucene or the query DSL, filter by field and time range, and edit documents as staged changes that you review before commit; on domains, an update is checked against the document version it read. Also runs SQL with JOIN and GROUP BY across indices and DynamoDB tables, k-NN vector search on knn_vector fields, CSV, JSON, and NDJSON import and export, and index creation. Proprietary, with a free plan that includes searching OpenSearch indices.
