**Tony Piazza** is a Senior Solutions Architect at Aiven in Austin, Texas. He works with customers from proof of concept through production, with a focus on OpenSearch for observability and AI/ML use cases. He writes technical blog posts, leads webinars, and speaks at meetups and conferences. He also contributes to the OpenSearch project across multiple repositories, including ML Commons, Data Prepper, Migration Assistant, and OpenSearch documentation. In Migration Assistant, he added support for Google Cloud.

Before Aiven, Tony built a 40+ year career in software architecture, data engineering, consulting, and technical training. He has trained thousands of software professionals on Java, distributed systems, and cloud-native development. He holds an M.S. in Computer Science from The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley.
