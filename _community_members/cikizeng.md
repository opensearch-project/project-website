**Ciki Zeng** is an independent builder who designs and verifies software built with AI agents. Her entry in the OpenSearch Agent Skills Hackathon, [unclosed](https://github.com/simpleciki/unclosed), won the 2026 Grand Prize. She builds IvyBloom, an adaptive learning system, and JumpOnion, a figure-skating jump analysis tool, and writes about evidence-first AI at [cikizeng.com](https://cikizeng.com).
